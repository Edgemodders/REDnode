REDnode runs mods written in JavaScript beside the game, and draws windows like this one over it. The tab at the bottom left opens the launcher; its Mod Manager switches mods on and off.

F10 moves REDnode through three modes:

ShowingREDnode's windows are drawn; the game keeps the mouse and keys. UsingThe mouse and keys work in REDnode's windows. Esc, or a click on empty screen, gives them back to the game. HiddenNothing of REDnode is drawn.

Careful: F9, right beside F10, is the game's Quick Load. Pressed by mistake, it loads your last save and you lose what you did since.
